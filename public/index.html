Apple Calendar

Events from these calendars show up on the TV automatically (they're read-only here; change them in Apple Calendar).

How to get the link

On iPhone: open Calendar, tap Calendars at the bottom, tap the ⓘ next to the calendar, turn on Public Calendar, then tap Share Link… and copy it.

On Mac: in Calendar, right-click the calendar in the sidebar, choose Share Calendar…, tick Public Calendar, and copy the link that appears.

The link starts with webcal://. Anyone with that link can see that calendar's events, so only share it here.
